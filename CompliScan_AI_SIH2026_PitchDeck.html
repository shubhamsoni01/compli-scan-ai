Slide 3 / 12 • Problem Analysis

Enforcement Bottlenecks & Consumer Vulnerability

Critical Gaps
1

Manual Inspection Crisis

Over 500M packaged units hit Indian shelves every month. Field officers physically inspect less than 0.01% due to extreme manpower shortages.

🚨 99.99% Goods Unchecked
2

Hidden & Micro Font Deceptions

Rule 7 of Legal Metrology mandates minimum 1mm to 6mm numeral heights relative to package area. Humans cannot visually distinguish 1.5mm vs 2.0mm fonts on store shelves.

⚠️ 42% Consumer Care Missing
3

Siloed Multi-Agency Rules

Food items require simultaneous compliance with Legal Metrology (MRP, USP, Net Qty) and FSSAI (FoSCoS license, Veg logo). Officers have no unified digital tool.

🏛️ 14+ Days to Draft Notices
Estimated ₹18,000+ Cr annual loss from deceptive packaging
